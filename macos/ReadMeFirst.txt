Discord Messenger for macOS
===========================

Drag "Discord Messenger" onto "Applications", then open it from there.

This app is not notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS refuses
to open it ("cannot be opened" or "Apple could not verify").  To open it
anyway, once:

  - Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.  Near the bottom it
    says "Discord Messenger" was blocked: click "Open Anyway" and
    confirm.  (On macOS 14 and earlier, Control-click the app in
    Applications and choose Open instead.)

After that it opens like any other app.

Logging in: scan the QR code with the Discord app, log in on discord.com's
own page in a window of the app, or use a token.  It follows your Mac's
light or dark appearance (or choose one under the gear's Theme menu).

Using third party clients is against Discord's terms of service.  The risk
of a ban is low, but it is there; the authors are not responsible for your
Discord account.
